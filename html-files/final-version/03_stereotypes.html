What the data says

Final takeaway

And we have our winners!

The data partially supports our hypothesis. After excluding TikTok, a strong runner-up appeared: Snapchat.

It looks like platforms where users can chat, see what friends interact with, and combine public content with personal messaging may be linked to higher scores across several indicators — especially the feeling of not being enough.

Surprisingly, Twitter and Reddit were expected to be more negative platforms, linked to feeling depressed, worried, or having trouble sleeping. However, both ranked lower on these questions, and Twitter was even the lowest for “bothered by worries”.
