### The Trend: Investigating the correlation between social media usage patterns and self-reported well-being.

- The Story: Telling a story about the "tipping point"—at what point does social media usage stop being "social" and start becoming detrimental to sleep and mood?
- The Data: \* Social Media and Mental Health Dataset (contains 20+ columns of survey data including screen time, platform used, and mental health scores).
- Technical Analysis (ML): \* Clustering (K-Means): Group users into "profiles" (e.g., "The Casual User," "The Doomscroller," "The Professional") based on their usage habits.
  Regression Analysis: Predict "Anxiety Scores" based on time spent on specific platforms.
- Visualizations (Website): \* Heatmap: A 2D grid showing the intensity of stress/anxiety relative to hours spent on TikTok vs. LinkedIn.
  Scrollytelling "Usage Path": As the user scrolls, show a character’s "mood meter" drop as their "simulated screen time" increases based on your data findings.
